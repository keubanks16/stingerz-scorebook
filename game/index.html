HOW TO PLAY

⚾ HITTING

Watch the pitcher. Tap the ball as it reaches the plate. Tap too early and you'll pull it foul; too late and you'll hit it the other way. On Pro and All-Star, where you tap matters too: tap above the ball for a grounder, below it for a fly ball.

POWER swings hit it harder but are harder to time. BUNT squares you up; tap to bunt.

🎯 PITCHING

Tap the strike zone to aim, then pick a pitch. A ring shrinks around your target: tap when it lines up for a perfect pitch. Mix fastballs with off-speed pitches to fool hitters.

🧤 FIELDING

Your fielders run to the ball on their own. When one of them catches it, tap the base to throw to. The glowing base is the smart play. If you wait too long, they'll make the throw themselves.

🏃 RUNNING

You're the base coach. During a play, tap GO ▶ to send a runner to the next base (tap again to keep him going) or ◀ BACK to send him back. ALL GO and ALL BACK move everyone. Forced runners always run.

Between pitches, tap STEAL on a runner. He takes off when the pitcher starts his delivery, and the catcher will try to throw him out.

On a fly ball, runners wait at the base. After the catch, tap GO to tag up. In Settings, Baserunning: Auto lets the runners decide (you can still send or hold them).
